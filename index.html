Who We Are?

RASMCO Group is a leading Saudi economic ecosystem established in 1987, pioneering strategic integration across Agriculture, Industry, and Real Estate. Aligned with Saudi Vision 2030, we operate +30 million m² of agricultural land to achieve year-round self-sufficiency with 100% natural, chemical-free produce and an annual yield exceeding 250,000 tons. Anchored by the Kingdom’s largest 33,000 m² industrial complex, a modern cold-chain logistics fleet, and a Zero Industrial Waste mandate, we combine scale with absolute sustainability. Today, we extend our expertise across neighboring Arab markets, delivering Saudi-made produce and industrial solutions built on trust, quality, and sustainable innovation.
